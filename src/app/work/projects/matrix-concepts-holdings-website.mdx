## Overview

The corporate website for Matrix Concepts Holdings Berhad (MCHB), celebrating 30 years of building freehold townships, landed homes and commercial developments across Malaysia. The site brings together the company's developments, news, investor relations, sustainability reports and scholarship programme in one place.

## Key Features

- **Our Developments**: Showcases MCHB's townships and projects, from Bandar Sri Sendayan in Seremban to the Klang Valley, Johor, Melbourne and Jakarta.
- **Investor Relations**: A dedicated section for shareholders and investors.
- **News & Sustainability**: Company news, sustainability updates and the scholarship programme.
- **Rich media experience**: A video-led home page with smooth, responsive layouts on desktop and mobile.
