## Overview

An online store for Jintian, a wellness brand selling the clinically studied Nu-Prep ERX supplement. Customers can buy once or subscribe, follow their progress in a personal dashboard, and read research-based articles.

## Key Features

- **Shop**: Product pages with Buy Once and monthly subscription options, priced in Malaysian ringgit.
- **Subscribe & Earn**: A subscription programme where the monthly price drops over time and customers earn free bottles. They can cancel, pause or resume at any time.
- **Customer Dashboard**: A "30 days Renewal Journey" that tracks each customer's progress.
- **Blog & Our Story**: Articles on sleep, energy and daily wellness, plus the brand's research background.
